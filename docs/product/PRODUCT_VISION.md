# HomeCenter Product Vision

HomeCenter is a local-first operating and information layer for home and family.

It combines existing systems, reduces them to useful everyday information, automates repetitive tasks and provides simple shared interfaces.

## Product promise
1. What do I need to know today?
2. Is anything unusual at home?
3. Do I need to take action?
4. Can HomeCenter safely handle a repetitive task for me?

## Product areas
- **HomeCenter Family** – shared dashboard, daily overview, calendar, shopping and simple actions
- **HomeCenter Automate** – Home Assistant, scenes, routines and deterministic automation
- **HomeCenter Ops** – servers, networking, backups, maintenance and diagnostics
- **HomeCenter Intelligence** – search, summaries, briefings, local voice and AI-assisted recommendations

## Principles
1. Family first
2. Local first
3. Integrate before replace
4. Manual control survives
5. Vendor-neutral domains
6. Progressive automation
7. No unnecessary hardware
8. Keep admin complexity away from family interfaces

```text
Observe -> Understand -> Assist -> Act -> Automate
```

AI may support interpretation, search and recommendations. It should not be the sole decision-maker for safety-critical functions.
