# Local AI and voice

## Goal
Natural interaction should improve convenience without making core home functions dependent on cloud AI or an LLM.

## Planned components
- local speech-to-text
- local text-to-speech
- music integration
- daily briefings
- search across home documentation
- energy and maintenance recommendations

## Safety rule
Simple home commands should remain deterministic. AI may explain, summarize and recommend, but safety-critical functions must operate within explicit rules and limits.
